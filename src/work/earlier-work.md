At Silverstripe I led design on websites for New Zealand government and public sector organisations, and often built their front ends. These sites have since been redesigned, so this is a record of the earlier versions I worked on rather than links to my work.

## Government and public sector

- Metlink, Greater Wellington's public transport journey planner
- Gisborne District Council
- Royal New Zealand College of General Practitioners
- Health Quality & Safety Commission
- Victims Information
- Electricity Authority
- Ako Panuku, for the Ministry of Education
- New Zealand Government Common Web Platform and its base theme
- Momentum sub-sites: Venator and RPM
