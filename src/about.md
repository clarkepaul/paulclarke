# About

I lead design across [Totara](https://www.totara.com/)'s learning platform. My work spans UX strategy, design systems, accessibility and the tooling that connects design to code. Most recently I have been moving our design practice to an AI-first way of working.

Before Totara, I spent more than ten years at [Silverstripe](https://www.silverstripe.com/). There I led a small UX team responsible for the experience and design of the open-source [Silverstripe CMS](https://www.silverstripe.org/) and [Silverstripe Cloud](https://www.silverstripe.com/cloud-hosting/), the company's hosting and deployment service.

At Silverstripe I spent a large part of my time building the Common Web Platform, an all-of-government service that helped New Zealand government agencies launch websites efficiently and securely. A major focus was an accessible base theme, which became one of the most widely used foundations for government websites over the following ten years.

I also worked on the agency side of the business, mostly for public sector clients: planning projects, running client consultation and design, and building the front ends for many of the websites we delivered.

I started out designing magazines, branding and marketing material, then moved into websites and digital products, including building their front ends. That range still shapes how I work: I care about the craft at every level, from type and layout to the code that ships it.

Some of my earlier writing and talks on design are collected on [my blog page](/blog.html).

## Outside work

I love a hands-on creative project. I am fitting out a bus as a motorhome for our family, and we share the build and our travels on [dustyandthesardines.nz](https://dustyandthesardines.nz). The bus and my motorbike are about the same age as me, so you could say I have a thing for retro things.

I spend as much time on the water as I can, surfing, kitesurfing or paddleboarding, after nine years following winter seasons to snowboard, mostly in the Austrian Alps. Most of my spare time now goes to family life with my kids, my wife and the dog, exploring New Zealand each summer.
