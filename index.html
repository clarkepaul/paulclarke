Head of Design.
I lead design teams and build the systems that let product design scale.

I lead design across Totara's learning platform. My work spans UX strategy, design systems, accessibility and the tooling that connects design to code. Most recently I have been moving our design practice to an AI-first way of working.

Before Totara, I spent more than ten years at Silverstripe, leading UX for the open-source CMS and Silverstripe Cloud, and helping build the all-of-government Common Web Platform.
